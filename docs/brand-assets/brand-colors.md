# NEOCARE Clinic - Brand Colors

Here are the official color codes and specifications for the NEOCARE Clinic brand system.

## Primary Colors

### 1. Crimson Red (Primary Brand Color)
Used for the primary logo, titles, and branding accents.
- **HEX:** `#7c3a3d`
- **RGB:** `R: 124, G: 58, B: 61` (also referenced as `R: 124, G: 33, B: 61` in CMYK conversion notes)
- **CMYK:** `C: 33, M: 78, Y: 58, K: 42`

### 2. Gold (Accent Color)
Used for accents, secondary logo elements, and highlights.
- **HEX:** `#cbab58`
- **RGB:** `R: 203, G: 171, B: 88`
- **CMYK:** `C: 20, M: 29, Y: 72, K: 6`

---

## Secondary & Fallback Colors

### 3. Terracotta Orange (Secondary Accent)
- **HEX:** `#914923`
- **RGB:** `R: 145, G: 73, B: 35`
- **CMYK:** `C: 30, M: 72, Y: 90, K: 30`

### 4. Dark Maroon / Dark Brown (Dark Mode Background / Text)
Used for dark mode backgrounds or deep text hierarchy.
- **HEX:** `#290d0d`
- **RGB:** `R: 41, G: 13, B: 13`

### 5. Warm Gray / Off-white (Light Mode Background)
Used for page backgrounds, card backdrops, and light surfaces.
- **HEX:** `#e3dbd9`
- **RGB:** `R: 227, G: 219, B: 217`
- **CMYK:** `C: 13, M: 14, Y: 13, K: 0`

### 6. Rich Black (Standard Text Fallback)
- **HEX:** `#000000`
- **RGB:** `R: 0, G: 0, B: 0`
- **CMYK:** `K: 100`
