# NEOCARE Clinic - Brand Applications & Contact Details

This document covers physical/digital applications of the NEOCARE brand, templates, and contact information.

---

## 1. NEOCARE Clinic Pattern
The brand pattern is based on a **circle** concept, symbolizing vitality and flexibility.
- Used in both print and digital applications.
- Layout height rules: Visual overlays ("Switch") are placed at **50%**, **55%**, or **65%** of the format height.
- The hero "Switch" is best placed in the middle (**50%**) or aligned to **55%** / **65%** of the format.

## 3. Digital Templates Guidelines

### Email Signature (توقيع البريد الإلكتروني)
- Consists of the sender name, role, contact numbers, email address, physical location, and the branding tagline.
- **Rules:** Do not recreate or change the email signature template structure.
- **Example layout:**
  ```text
  Dr. [Doctor Name]
  CEO
  Tel: [Phone] | Fax: [Fax]
  E-Mail: Email@neocare.clinic
  P.O.Box: Road Name Rd, Kuwait City, Kuwait.
  
  Your health is our priority | صحتك هي أولويتنا
  Call now | اتصل الآن
  NEOCARE CLINIC
  ```

### Social Media (حسابات التواصل)
- Profiles should use the official standalone emblem as the avatar.
- Bios should include the official clinic tagline, location, and website link (`neocare.clinic`).

### PPT & Word Templates
- Presentations should use the IBM Plex Sans Arabic font.
- Follow the heading and color hierarchy (Crimson Red for main headings, Gold/Gray for sublines, and Off-White/White backgrounds).
