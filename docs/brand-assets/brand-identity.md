# NEOCARE Clinic - Brand Identity & Logo Guidelines

This document outlines the usage of the NEOCARE Clinic logo, emblem, clear space, minimum size, and misusage rules.

---

## 1. NEOCARE Clinic Logo
The brand logo represents the core of the visual identity. It consists of the wordmark and the emblem lock-up.

- **Primary Version:** Full color logo containing the emblem and "NEOCARE" wordmark, along with the tagline "Premium Medical Service" (Arabic: الخدمة الطبية المتميزة).
- **Secondary Version:** Alternative layout configuration of the logo.

> **Rule:** Never redraw, modify, or design the logo. Always use official vector master files.

---

## 2. Emblem (الشعار الرمز)
The NEOCARE Emblem is a standalone brand element as well as part of the logo.
- Can be used independently as the reduced form of the logo in tight spaces (e.g., app icons, avatars).
- Can be placed on white, dark background colors, tints, and light/bright images.

---

## 3. Sizing & Safe Space

### Clear Space
- Keep a clear margin/safe space around the logo equal to **2X** the height of the word "NEOCARE".
- Keep an outer margin of **50%** of the logo's size as a general guideline.

### Minimum Sizes
To guarantee visibility and legibility:
- **Logo (Full Wordmark + Emblem):**
  - **Print:** Minimum height of **10mm**.
  - **Digital:** Minimum height of **35px**.
- **Emblem (Standalone):**
  - **Print:** Minimum height of **5mm**.
  - **Digital:** Minimum height of **25px**.

---

## 4. Logo Misusage (الاستخدامات الخاطئة للشعار)
To maintain brand integrity, avoid the following misusages:
- **Do not stretch or skew** the logo (ال تقم بتغيير نسب الشعار).
- **Do not create new lock-ups** or change the proportions of the elements (ال تقم بإنشاء أقفال جديدة أو تغيير نسب العناصر).
- **Do not change the position** of the logotype relative to the emblem (ال تقم بتغيير كتلة من مكانها).
- **Do not change the colors** of the logo (ال تقم بتغيير ألوان الشعار).
- **Do not apply drop shadows** or 3D effects (ال تقم بإنشاء ظل أو تغيير شكل الشعار إلى شكل ثلاثي الأبعاد).
- **Do not rotate, distort, or flip** the logo (ال تقم بتدوير الشعار).
- **Do not use low contrast backgrounds**; always ensure readability (ال تقم بوضع الشعار مباشرة على خلفية غير متباينة).
- **Do not apply the full-color logo** on solid colors other than the primary brand palette.
